What we do

Muffler, Exhaust & Brake Repair

The name says it all. We focus on the systems that keep your car quiet, clean-running and stopping safely.

  • Muffler Replacement

    Rusted, rattling or blown-out muffler? We'll get your vehicle back to running quiet.

  • Exhaust Repair

    Leaks, broken hangers, damaged pipes and tailpipes, fixed so fumes go where they should.

  • Noise Diagnosis

    Loud rumble, hissing or rattling underneath? We'll track down where it's coming from.

  • Brake Pads

    Squealing, grinding or soft pedal feel? Fresh pads bring back smooth, confident stops.

  • Rotors & Brake Repair

    Rotor service and replacement, plus repairs for vibration, pulling and other brake issues.

  • Inspections

    Not sure what that sound is? Bring it in for a brake and exhaust check before it gets worse.
